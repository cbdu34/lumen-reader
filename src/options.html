The quiet web

Ada Lovelace · example.com · 4 min read

Reading on the web is a design problem before it is a technological one. Strip the page to its sentences, give them a measure and a rhythm, and attention does the rest.

Lumen keeps the words, drops the noise, and never asks for an account.

Privacy

  • No network calls. Lumen never sends anything anywhere. There is no analytics, no sync server, no crash reporting.
  • No host permissions. Lumen cannot read any page until you click its button on that page, thanks to activeTab.
  • Local storage only. Your theme, text size and column width live in chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome may sync between your own devices.
  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for.
